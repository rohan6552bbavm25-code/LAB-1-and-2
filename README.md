This project is based on the use of Generative AI in the aviation industry, with a focus on improving business communication and passenger experience. The project explains how AI tools such as ChatGPT and Google Gemini can be used in aviation while also highlighting the importance of human review and verification.

The project covers the difference between Traditional AI and Generative AI. Traditional AI is mainly used for prediction, classification, detection, recommendation and forecasting, while Generative AI is used to create new content such as emails, summaries, advertisements, reports and presentation material. It also explains how both types of AI can work together in an airline workflow.

A major part of the project focuses on retrieved and generated information. Real aviation information should be taken from reliable sources, while AI can be used to convert verified information into useful passenger communication. The project also demonstrates structured prompting using role, context, task, constraints and output format.

The fictional airline business case, AeroVista Airways, focuses on flight-disruption communication. The project shows how AI can help create passenger emails, announcements and other communication during flight delays. It also includes an AI-supported workflow involving verified information, prompt design, AI generation, human review, source verification and final approval.

Real aviation data and regulatory information are included to connect the project with the actual aviation industry. The project also considers important risks such as hallucination, outdated information, privacy issues and incorrect policy claims.

Overall, the project demonstrates that Generative AI can improve the speed, clarity and efficiency of aviation business communication, but important operational and passenger-facing decisions should remain subject to verification and human approval.
